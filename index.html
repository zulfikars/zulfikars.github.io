I am a researcher from Indonesia. My intellectual formation began at Qatar University, where I earned a B.A. in International Affairs with a concentration in Middle East and Islamic politics. I went on to complete an M.A. in International Politics and a Ph.D. in Politics at the University of Manchester in the UK, which I completed at the age of 26.

My research examines three interlocking relationships: China-Indonesia, Indonesia-Middle East, and China-Middle East. I approach them as a single relational system in which economic interests, strategic calculation, and cultural influence continually shape one another accross regions.

I analyse these ties accross three domains: the political economy of investment, trade, and technology; diplomatic, military, and security cooperation; and soft power, encompassing media, education, culture, and people-to-people exchange. I also study the narratives through which external actors seek to shape public perceptions, and how these are received, contested, or amplified domestically. Rather than treating "China," "Indonesia," or "the Middle East" as unitary actors, I disaggregate them into the ministries, sovereign wealth funds, firms, political parties, religious organisations, universities, media outlets, and influencers through which engagement is actually conducted. I also attend to the subnational scale, exploring how provincial and local governments partner with foreign actors and how influence is negotiated unevenly accross regions.

At the moment, I also dedicate part of my time exploring the global politics of Indonesia's energy transition. This work asks who finances the transition, who benefits from it, and who bears its costs. It looks at the role of Chinese, MENA, and wider international capital, the governance of critical minerals and clean energy supply chains, and the social and environmental consequences of investment in the regions where these projects are built.

I currently serve as the Director of the China-Indonesia Desk and the Indonesia-MENA Desk at the Center of Economic and Law Studies (CELIOS) in Jakarta.

At the same time, I am also a research affiliate at the Middle East Institute-National University of Singapore and a visiting researcher at Busan University of Foreign Studies, where I was a research professor between 2022 and 2025.

Previously, I served as an assistant professor at Universitas Islam Indonesia and as a research associate at Jakarta-based Institute for Development of Economics and Finance (INDEF). I have taught at Universitas Gadjah Mada, Universitas Airlangga, Bina Nusantara University, Universitas Paramadina, and Universitas Kristen Indonesia.

Between 2022 and 2024, I was also an associate at the London School of Economics' IDEAS.

Additionally, I have undertaken consulting assignments for a range of organisations, including Oxfam, USAID, Wikistrat, Gulf State Analytics, Freedom House, Indonesia's Ministry of Finance, Grapevine Asia Partners, Doublethink Lab, Dialectica, among others. I am also regularly quoted in leading newspapers, including New York Times, South China Morning Post, Nikkei Asia Review, Voice of America, Al Jazeera, Radio Free Asia, and others.

In recognition of my scholarship, I received the Outstanding Research award from Qatar National Research Fund in 2015 and was named Most Productive Author by The Conversation Indonesia in both 2020 and 2022.

Between 2014-2019, I contributed to a writing project examining global Muslim affairs around the world, while from 2015 to 2020, I participated in a research initiative on Indonesia's socio-economic landscape. These projects were funded by Qatar and UK-based institutions respectively. Their findings, published as media articles, are listed on this site.

As a person with disability, I am also deeply engaged in disability-related issues. Apart from occassionally writing on the subject, I lead Sekolabilitas, an Indonesian-based NGO committed to ensuring children with disabilities have access to quality education. I have shared my personal journey in a book written in Indonesian, along with a summarized version available in both Indonesian and in English.

Since 2020, however, I have chosen to focus exclusively on writing, interviews, and projects aligned my core research interests.

In my free time, I enjoy hiking and computer programming.
